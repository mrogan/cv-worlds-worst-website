# The shop: a brief

Every page, product and error message is written from this page. If a line of copy could not have come from the man described here, it is wrong.

## The shop

**Mossop's Practical Sundries**, of Lower Thrumble. "Honest goods, honestly described."

A one-man shop, run from a garage, selling about thirty things its owner believes are useful. He is nearly right about most of them. Each is described with complete honesty, which is where the trouble starts.

## The proprietor

**Gerald Mossop** spent 31 years in the signage department of Thrumble District Council and retired in the spring. He opened the shop in March. He is proud of it, careful, and incapable of overselling: if a ladder has one rung, the listing says so, and then explains what one rung is good for.

The website was built by his nephew **Darren**, who is doing a course. Gerald thinks it is marvellous. Anything it cannot do yet is "phase two".

Neither of them knows the shop is funny.

## What it sells

Ordinary objects with one thing wrong, sold as though that were a feature or at least not a problem. Prices are low, exact and in pounds. For example:

| Product | Gerald says |
|---|---|
| Jigsaw, 999 pieces | "A 1,000-piece jigsaw of Thrumble Viaduct. One piece is not present. Priced accordingly." |
| Ladder, one rung | "Suitable for low shelves and modest ambitions." |
| Calendar, last year's | "Every date in it has been verified." |
| Clock, stopped | "Correct twice daily, which is more than can be said for some." |
| Glove, left | "For customers who already have the right one." |
| Candle, lightly used | "Lit once, for a test. It passed." |
| Key, spare | "We have not yet found the lock. When we do, it will be listed separately." |
| Doorstop | "It is a brick. We see no reason to pretend otherwise." |
| Batteries, flat (pack of 4) | "Ideal for testing a battery tester." |
| Battery tester | "See *Batteries, flat*." |

Four departments: **Home**, **Workshop**, **Desk** and **Outdoors**. Each product has a name in the catalogue style ("Glove, left"), an item number, a price, a short description, a longer "Gerald's notes", and how many are in stock (often one).

There is no basket. To buy something you write to Gerald through the contact form and he sets it aside. A basket is phase two.

## How it talks

- **Gerald writes everything**, as "we". Plain, slightly formal British English. Short sentences. Full stops.
- **Honest to a fault.** He states the flaw first, then defends the product on its merits. He never apologises for stock and never exaggerates.
- **Proud of small things.** The shop "has gone well, considering". A product that sold once is "popular".
- **Specific.** Real-sounding numbers, places and dates: item 14, the viaduct, a Tuesday. Never "various" or "and more".
- **No winking.** No puns, no exclamation marks, no jokes about websites, bugs, computers or AI. The site never admits to being bad, and never mentions the factory. The joke is always the object, or Gerald's sincerity about it.
- **Kind.** Nobody is the butt of it: not customers, not Darren, not Lower Thrumble.

Errors and empty states are Gerald too. Not found: "We have looked, and we do not stock that page." Nothing in a search: "Nothing matches. We have noted the gap in our range." Message sent: "Thank you. Gerald reads every message, usually on a Thursday."

## How it looks

Tidy and plain, like a well-kept parish newsletter: cream paper, dark green ink, one serif for headings, generous space. It is *not* ugly, loud or broken-looking. A visitor should think "sweet little shop" before reading a word, so that anything wrong with a page stands out as wrong.

Products are illustrated with Gerald's own line drawings, because the camera was sold in April (item 14).

Accessible to WCAG 2.2 AA, readable on a phone, system fonts, no third-party requests.

## Pages

| Page | What is on it |
|---|---|
| Home | A welcome from Gerald, the four departments, and "This week's sundries": four products. |
| Products | The whole range, twelve to a page, filtered by department. |
| A product | Drawing, name, item number, price, stock, description, Gerald's notes, and "Customers also considered". |
| Search | One box. Results as on the product list. |
| About | Gerald, the garage, Darren, and a short history of the shop since March. |
| Contact | Name, email and message. It thanks the sender. It stores and sends nothing. |

Every page carries "Report a problem": one box, for telling the shop that something on the page is wrong.

## Names

The repository is `cv-worlds-worst-website`, and its README explains the joke. The site itself is only ever "Mossop's Practical Sundries".
